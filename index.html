Download the model

Clem is voiced by a small AI language model that runs entirely on your device. You'll download it once (about 500–800 MB, depending on your browser), and your browser saves it for next time. After that, nothing you type is sent to any server, and the chat even works offline.

Starting…

Works best in a recent Chrome or Edge on a computer (uses your graphics card with WebGPU). Other browsers fall back to a slower mode. Use Wi-Fi if you can.
